How do I book a ticket?

Enter From and To on the home page, press Search, choose a bus and seats, then pay. You get a ticket with a booking ID straight away.

Can I choose my seat?

Yes. The seat map shows live availability. Booked seats are greyed out.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Enter the passenger names while booking. Each traveller must carry a photo ID.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open My Trips, select the upcoming trip and choose Cancel. The refund amount is shown before you confirm.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go to the original payment method. UPI and wallets are usually quick. Cards can take 5 to 7 working days.

What if the operator cancels my bus?

You get a full refund automatically, or you can rebook another bus for free.

How do I track my bus?

Open Track Bus from the menu. The map shows live position and estimated arrival. Tracking is an estimate and can vary with traffic.

My bus is late. What should I do?

You get an alert when the bus runs late. If you will miss the bus, contact support at once.

Which payment methods work?

UPI, Visa, Mastercard and RuPay cards, and wallets. Manage saved cards in Payment Methods.

Money was deducted but I have no ticket.

The amount is reversed automatically within 5 to 7 working days. If it is not, contact us with the payment reference.

How do I use a coupon?

Copy a code from the Offers section and paste it at checkout. One coupon per booking. Try TRACKB20 on your first trip.

I forgot my password.

On the login page choose the password reset option and follow the steps sent to your email.

How do I change my details or theme?

Edit your name, email and phone in My Profile. Change Light, Dark or System theme in Settings.
