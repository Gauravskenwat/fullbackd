Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Sample policy for this project. Have it reviewed before you go live.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number.
  • Booking details: routes, seats, passenger names and trip history.
  • Payment references. We never store full card numbers or CVV.
  • Location, only if you tap "my location" on the tracking map.

How we use it

  • To confirm bookings, send tickets and alerts about your trips.
  • To keep payments safe and prevent fraud.
  • To improve routes and the app. Offers are sent only if you subscribe.

Sharing

We share passenger details with the operator running your trip and with payment providers. We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices

  • Edit your details in My Profile.
  • Turn alerts on or off in Notifications.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link in any email.
  • Ask us to delete your account at support@smartbus.in.

Storage in your browser

This site saves your theme, saved routes and chat history in your browser's local storage. Clear it any time from your browser settings.
