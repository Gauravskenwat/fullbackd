1. Using TrackB

TrackB is a platform that lets you search, book and track bus services run by independent operators. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and you are responsible for keeping your login details safe.

2. Bookings

A booking is confirmed when you get a ticket with a booking ID. Carry a valid government photo ID. Boarding points and times are set by the operator, so arrive at least 15 minutes early.

3. Cancellation & refunds

  • Cancel from My Trips before departure.
  • The refund depends on how early you cancel, and the operator's policy shown at booking applies.
  • Refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
  • If an operator cancels a trip, you get a full refund.

4. Payments & offers

Fares include taxes unless stated. Only one coupon can be used per booking, and coupons cannot be exchanged for cash. We may withdraw an offer that is misused.

5. Liability

Operators provide the journey. Live tracking and arrival times are estimates and can change with traffic and weather. Our liability is limited to the amount you paid for the booking, to the extent the law allows.
