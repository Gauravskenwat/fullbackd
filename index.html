Frequently asked questions

How do I track my bus?

Open Track Bus from the menu or the bottom bar and enter your booking. The map updates live with the bus position and estimated arrival.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Yes. Go to History, open the trip and choose Cancel. Refund timing depends on how close to departure you cancel.

How do I use a coupon code?

Copy a code from the offers above and paste it in the promo field at checkout. Only one code can be used per booking.

Which payment methods are supported?

UPI, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay) and saved payment methods from your profile.
